MORE THAN A ROLE

Become the
all-round actor.

Great acting goes beyond memorising lines. It means understanding the camera, the visual world, the people on set, and the decisions that bring a story to life.

Beyond the Performance gives actors a wider view of filmmaking—so you can show up prepared, collaborate confidently, and build a career with intention.

WHAT YOU'LL EXPLORE

Learn the craft.
Know the industry.

Five practical learning areas designed to help you grow on screen and beyond it.

01✳

Acting & Cinematography

Understand framing, shot sizes, eyelines, blocking, continuity, and how to work with the camera.

02◈

Production Design

Explore how sets, props, costumes, colours, and visual atmosphere shape a character and a story.

03▤

Production Management

Get familiar with schedules, call sheets, rehearsals, set etiquette, and professional preparation.

04◎

The Dynamics of Casting

Learn about auditions, self-tapes, callbacks, chemistry reads, role preparation, and casting choices.

05↗

Branding for Actors

Build your professional identity, portfolio, showreel, visibility, network, and career positioning.

WALK AWAY WITH

More confidence.
More perspective.

  • ✓ A stronger understanding of screen performance
  • ✓ Better collaboration with film production teams
  • ✓ More confidence approaching auditions and casting
  • ✓ A clearer professional identity and actor brand
  • ✓ Practical insight into the filmmaking process
